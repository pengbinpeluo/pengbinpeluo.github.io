# We Are Hiring

The <strong>Water, Agriculture, and Conservation Innovation Lab (WACI Lab)</strong>, led by Dr. Bin Peng at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, is recruiting postdoctoral researchers and graduate students working on the water-agriculture nexus from microscale to macroscale — spanning plant-soil and plant-soil-microbe interactions, field-to-watershed hydrology and water quality, satellite and UAV remote sensing, AI for crop, soil, and environmental sciences, environmental data science, and agriculture in the Earth system.

For current open positions, detailed descriptions of each research area, and application instructions, see the **[WACI Lab hiring page]({{ site.waci_lab_url }})**.
